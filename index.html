Who We Are

Direct Lender.
Trusted Partner.

1Oak Lending is a vertically integrated, family-backed investment firm focused on structuring and investing in diversified portfolios of short-term, senior-secured loans collateralized by U.S. commercial real estate.

Disciplined capital deployment, direct lending, and strategic real estate investments designed to deliver consistent yield enhancement and downside protection across market cycles.

"If your situation falls outside a standard box, we structure a solution that fits."

Lending Programs

Purpose-Built
Financing

We underwrite to future performance — factoring in renovations, lease-up efforts, operational improvements, and capital initiatives that will enhance cash flow and asset value. Our loans are purpose-built to solve problems, not to fit within traditional underwriting boxes.

Acquisition Loan
Acquire &
Execute

Designed for owners and operators executing a business plan to acquire, improve, reposition, or stabilize a property before refinancing, holding, or selling. We underwrite to future performance ahead of the property qualifying for traditional financing.

Security: First mortgage lien or deed of trust. Min. 9-month interest on 12-month terms. Appraisal + Phase 1 required.

Bridge Loan
Bridge
the Gap

Short-term financing for non-standard, time-sensitive, or complex situations where conventional lenders are unable or unwilling to proceed — imminent maturity, foreclosure pressure, capital shortfalls, partnership disputes, or temporary cash-flow disruption.

Loan Amount$1M – $25M
Term12 Months
LTV / LTC70% / 80%
RateFixed, 10% – 14%
AmortizationInterest-Only
Origination1% – 2%
Timeline10 – 30 Days
Property TypesAll Commercial Asset Classes

Security: First mortgage lien or deed of trust. Broadest collateral eligibility — we lend where the underlying real estate remains fundamentally sound.

Construction Loan
Build &
Develop

Specialized financing for ground-up development and major adaptive reuse. Structured around a defined development plan, budget, and timeline — carries the project through completion and stabilization before transitioning into permanent financing or sale.
