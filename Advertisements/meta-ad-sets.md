# 1Oak Lending — Meta Ad Media Sets

Five campaign concepts targeting CRE brokers (Referral & Broker Program) and borrowers (bridge / value-add / acquisition loans). Each set includes targeting direction, creative format, primary copy, headline, description, and CTA.

---

## AD SET 1 — "Broker Paycheck" (Broker Referral Acquisition)

**Objective:** Lead Generation
**Audience:**
- Job titles: Commercial Real Estate Broker, CRE Broker, Investment Sales Broker, Mortgage Broker, Real Estate Advisor
- Interests: Commercial real estate, LoopNet, CoStar, real estate investment
- Geography: Kansas City, Indianapolis, Columbus, Cincinnati, Louisville, Nashville, Memphis, Raleigh, Minneapolis, Detroit, Atlanta, St. Louis, Boise, Quad Cities
- Age: 28–55

**Format:** Single image (1080×1080) or short Reel (9:16, 15–30 sec)

**Visual Direction:**
Static — clean, minimal dark forest green background. Large serif number "$25,000" center-frame in gold/tan. Below it in small mono caps: "ON A $2.5M DEAL. ZERO LOAN MANAGEMENT." Bottom right: 1Oak wordmark.
Video — screen recording-style counter animating from $0 → $25,000 with a "deal closed" timestamp appearing. Ends on 1Oak logo lockup on forest background.

**Primary Copy:**
> You sourced the deal. Your client needs capital. You don't have a lender who can actually close.
>
> 1Oak Lending pays a 1% referral fee on every funded loan — bridge, value-add, or acquisition. You stay close to your client. We handle the financing.
>
> No origination. No underwriting. No babysitting the loan.
>
> If you're a CRE broker with a book of business that runs into financing friction — this is the program.

**Headline:** Earn 1% on Every Deal You Send Us

**Description:** CRE bridge & value-add lender. $1M–$25M. Close in 30 days.

**CTA Button:** Learn More → broker-program landing page

## Production Notes

**Pixels to install before launch:**
- Meta Pixel on index.html, broker-program/index.html, lender-program/index.html
- Standard events: PageView, Lead (on form submit), ViewContent (on #submit scroll)

**Copy variants to A/B test per set:**
- Headline: benefit-led vs. urgency-led (e.g. "Earn 1% Per Deal" vs. "Stop Leaving Referral Fees on the Table")
- CTA: "Learn More" vs. "Get Started" vs. "Submit Your Deal"

**Recommended launch order:**
1. Set 2 (borrower cold) — largest audience, fastest feedback on CPL
2. Set 1 (broker cold) — targeted, lower volume but high LTV per conversion
3. Set 5 (borrower retargeting) — re-engage warm traffic immediately
4. Set 4 (broker retargeting) — needs pixel data, launch after 2 weeks
5. Set 3 (value-add) — test after validating Set 2 messaging
